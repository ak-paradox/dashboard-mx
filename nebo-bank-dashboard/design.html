Design principles

One accent job

Coral is reserved for progress, active chart segments, CTAs, level, and notification dots — never for decorative chrome.

Depth encodes affordance

Raised = content container. Inset = tappable tile or track. Pressing a raised control temporarily insets it.

Theme preserves motif

Light mode keeps radii, Inter, and accent. Only surface luminance and shadow polarity change via CSS variables.

Quiet motion

Fade-up on cards, soft accent glow on motifs, chart line draw — no continuous noise or stacked parallax.
